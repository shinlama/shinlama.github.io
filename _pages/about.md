<p class="intro-lede">I build and study AI systems for spatial data and design. My current interests are <strong>spatial computing</strong>, <strong>3D vision</strong>, and <strong>human-centered AI</strong>.</p>

I am an M.S. student in Interior Architecture at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Jin-Kook Lee. My work connects machine learning methods with usable prototypes, including indoor 3D reconstruction, generative models for architectural images, and text-based analysis of spatial experience.
